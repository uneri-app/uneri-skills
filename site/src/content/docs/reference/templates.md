---
title: Templates
description: What is in uneri-templates/.
---

`plugins/uneri-skills/uneri-templates/` holds the scaffolds for `.kiro/uneri/`: `use-cases.md`, `domain-model.md`, `actors.md` and `glossary.md`.
They are not placed automatically. `/uneri-skills:udd-init` generates them in your project's language.
