The author's approach to use cases was shaped by Doug Rosenberg and Matt Stephens, *Use Case Driven Object Modeling with UML: Theory and Practice* (Apress, 2007).
The user-goal level of a use case is Alistair Cockburn's, from *Writing Effective Use Cases*.

The SDD skills are ported from the `kiro-*` skills of [cc-sdd](https://github.com/gotalab/cc-sdd) (MIT) and changed for use-case-driven development; they build on
Kiro's spec-driven development. The cc-sdd copyright notice and licence are in [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](https://github.com/uneri-app/uneri-skills/blob/main/plugins/uneri-skills/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). uneri's skills are self-contained and need no other plugin at runtime.

Kiro is a product of Amazon Web Services. uneri-skills follows its `.kiro/` directory layout and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Amazon Web
Services or cc-sdd.
