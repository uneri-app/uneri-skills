## When Claude picks it

This is the skill's own description, which Claude reads to decide when to use it:

> Draft a new Architecture Decision Record (ADR), audit existing ones against the authoring conventions (status consistency, no progress or ticket numbers in the body, decision granularity, immutability), or consult them before changing code. Use when writing, reviewing, superseding, or accepting an ADR, when asked whether the ADRs are well written, or when about to make a change that an accepted ADR may govern.
